Our Mission

At our cafe, we are on a mission to nurture both people and the planet through delicious organic food. Join us on our journey towards sustainability, one meal at a time.

Future Goals

We're committed to expanding our eco-friendly practices, introducing innovative menu options, and empowering our community to make healthier choices. Together, let's build a greener, healthier future.

Where to Find Us

We are located in the Scottish Borders, near Kelso. There are signposts and roadsigns you can follow in and around Kelso to visit our Cafe & Farm.

If you aren't up for the adventure, our address is 123 Organic Way, Kelso, Scottish Borders. EH33 4TY.
